Follow your Tibber Grid Rewards in Homey, in real time.

See whether your home is available for Grid Rewards, when it delivers and why, and what you earn per session, today, yesterday, this month and in total. While a session runs you get an estimate, and afterwards you see what Tibber's settlement added. Everything is logged to Insights, so you can follow your earnings over time.

Add your electric car and home battery as their own devices. See what each of them earns, how much of the day it is available, and its best day and best session. For the car: whether it is plugged in and smart charging is on, a daily reminder if it is not plugged in, and a check against your charger that warns when Tibber has lost contact with the car. For the battery: its savings, what it is doing right now, its planned charge level until the end of tomorrow, and its day: hours for Grid Rewards, price and solar, and energy in and out.

Use Flow cards to act on it: when a session starts or ends, a daily and monthly summary, when today's reward passes an amount, when a record is set, or when Grid Rewards has been unavailable for a few hours, for example because the car is not plugged in.

Three dashboard widgets show the status, the last 7 days and the battery's plan, and update as soon as something changes. The history page in the app settings shows every session with its reason and device, totals per day and per reason, and records, and exports it all as CSV.

You log in with your Tibber e-mail and password. The password is stored on your Homey and only sent to Tibber.

This is an unofficial app. It is not made or supported by Tibber, and it uses Tibber's app API, which can change without notice.
