Follow your Tibber Grid Rewards in Homey, in real time.

See whether your home is available for Grid Rewards, when it delivers and why, and what you earn per session, today, this month and in total. Everything is logged to Insights, so you can follow your earnings over time.

Add your electric car and home battery as their own devices. See whether the car is plugged in and smart charging is on, and for the battery its savings, what it is doing right now and its next planned charge or discharge.

Use Flow cards to act on it: when a session starts or ends, a daily and monthly summary, when today's reward passes an amount, or when Grid Rewards has been unavailable for a few hours, for example because the car is not plugged in. A dashboard widget and a session history page show it all at a glance.

You log in with your Tibber e-mail and password. The password is stored on your Homey and only sent to Tibber.

This is an unofficial app. It is not made or supported by Tibber, and it uses Tibber's app API, which can change without notice.
